Privacy Policy

How we handle your information — in plain words

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Shiekh AC Repair ("we", "our") is a local AC repair workshop in Razaabad, Faisalabad. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it. No legal jargon — just straight answers.

What information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, phone number, the service you need, and whatever you write in the message box.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address.
  • WhatsApp chats: whatever you send us when you message our WhatsApp number.

That's it. We don't run accounts, we don't track you across the internet, and we don't buy data from anyone.

How we use it

  • To call or message you back about your AC problem — that's the whole point of the contact form.
  • To send occasional AC maintenance tips and seasonal offers, only if you subscribed to the newsletter. You can unsubscribe anytime by replying to any email or just telling us on WhatsApp.

What we never do

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing. Your phone number stays with us. The only exception is if the law requires us to share it — which, for an AC repair shop, has never happened.

Cookies

Our website itself doesn't set tracking cookies. Our hosting provider (Netlify/Vercel) may keep basic server logs (like which pages were visited) to keep the site running — that's standard for every website and we never see individual identities in it.

Your rights

Want to know what information we have about you, or want it deleted? Just call +92 303 6253428 or email info@Shiekhacrepair.com and we'll sort it out.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
