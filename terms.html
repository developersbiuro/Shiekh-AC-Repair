The fair rules we work by

Last updated: November 4, 2026

These terms cover AC repair, installation, gas refilling, and maintenance services provided by Shiekh AC Repair, Razaabad, Faisalabad. By booking a visit, you agree to the following — all written the way we'd explain them at your doorstep.

Quotes and pricing

We diagnose first and quote before starting any paid work. The price we agree on is the price you pay — no hidden charges added afterwards. If we discover additional faults mid-repair, we stop, explain, and only continue with your approval.

Warranty

Every repair carries a warranty on our workmanship and on the parts we replace. The exact duration depends on the job — ask before we start and we'll write it on your bill. Warranty doesn't cover new, unrelated faults or damage caused after our visit (power surges, mishandling, etc.).

Appointments

We aim for same-day visits across Faisalabad, but times are estimates, not guarantees — traffic and emergency jobs can shift the schedule. If we're running late, we'll call and tell you.

Your part

Please make sure our technician can safely reach the indoor and outdoor units, and that someone over 18 is present during the visit. Accurate information about the problem (brand, what's happening) helps us bring the right parts the first time.

Fair limits

We're honest about what we can and can't fix. If your AC genuinely isn't worth repairing — say, a dead compressor in a 12-year-old unit — we'll tell you so rather than sell you a repair. Our liability for any single job is limited to the amount you paid for that job.

Website content

Blog articles and guides on this site are general advice, not a substitute for a technician looking at your specific unit. Prices mentioned anywhere are indicative — your quote is confirmed in person.
