Our Story

One toolbox, one van, zero shortcuts

Started in Razaabad, Built on Word of Mouth

Shiekh AC Repair began with one technician, one toolbox, and a second-hand van in Razaabad. No investors, no franchise boards — just a simple rule: fix it like it's your own home's AC.

In Faisalabad, where summer crosses 45°C, a properly repaired AC on a brutal June afternoon advertises itself. Neighbours told neighbours, and that's how we grew — one honest repair at a time. Today we handle split and inverter ACs of every common brand — Gree, Dawlance, Haier, Kenwood, Orient, PEL — across the city, but the rule hasn't changed.
