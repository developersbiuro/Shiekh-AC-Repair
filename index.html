AC Repair

Warm air, water dripping inside, strange noises, breaker tripping — we find the real cause (gas leak, dead capacitor, faulty PCB, choked coil) and fix it at your place. Most jobs finish in a single visit.

AC Installation

New split or inverter AC? We mount it right — solid brackets, proper copper piping, full vacuuming before charging gas — so it cools faster and lives longer. We also shift ACs when you move house.

Learn More

Gas Refilling

Low gas means weak cooling and heavy bills. We don't just top it up — we find the leak, seal it, then refill R22 or R410A to factory pressure. That's the only way a refill actually lasts.

Learn More

AC Service & Maintenance

Dust-choked filters and coils make your AC eat electricity. Our full service washes everything — filters, coils, blower, drain tray — so it cools like new and the air smells clean.
