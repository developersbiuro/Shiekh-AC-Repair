Inverter AC vs Normal Split AC: Which Should You Buy in Pakistan?

October 5, 2026  •  Shiekh AC Repair, Faisalabad

Walk into any AC shop in Faisalabad and you'll hear it: "Inverter le lein, bijli bachegi." It's mostly true — but not always worth it. Here's the honest breakdown from people who repair both, every day.

How an inverter AC actually saves power

A normal (non-inverter) compressor is binary: full blast or off. It blasts, the room cools, it switches off, the room warms, it blasts again. An inverter compressor slows down once the room is cool and just maintains the temperature — using far less electricity. On 8–12 hour daily runs, expect 30–40% lower consumption.

When inverter is worth the extra money

  • Bedrooms or lounges used 8+ hours daily — the savings pay back the price difference in 2–3 summers.
  • Rooms where you hate temperature swings — inverters hold a steadier temperature and run quieter.
  • New construction — you're buying fresh anyway, so buy the efficient one.

When a normal split is the smarter buy

  • Guest rooms or shops used 2–3 hours a day — you'll never recover the price difference.
  • Tight budget — a good 1.5-ton non-inverter from Gree or Dawlance cools just as well; it just costs more to run.
  • Areas with heavy voltage fluctuation and no stabilizer — inverter PCBs are expensive to replace if they fry. (A voltage stabilizer or protector is cheap insurance for either type.)

The repair-cost reality nobody mentions

Inverter ACs have sophisticated circuit boards. When they fail out of warranty, a PCB replacement costs several times more than any part in a normal split. This doesn't mean avoid inverters — it means buy a reputable brand (Gree, Dawlance, Haier, Kenwood) with service availability in Faisalabad, and get it installed properly. Most "inverter problems" we see are actually bad installations.

Our honest recommendation

Main bedroom or lounge, long daily use → inverter, 1.5 ton, reputable brand. Occasional-use room → quality non-inverter and spend the savings on proper installation. Either way, get it installed by someone who vacuums the lines properly — that's what decides how long any AC lives.
