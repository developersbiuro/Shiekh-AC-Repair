SHIEKH AC REPAIR — WEBSITE (static version)
===========================================
5 pages: Home (index.html), About Us, Services, Contact Us, FAQs.

VIEW LOCALLY
Just open index.html in any browser.

PUT IT ONLINE — NETLIFY (free, easiest)
1. Unzip this file.
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag the unzipped
   folder "shiekh-ac-repair-website" onto the page.
3. You get a live link instantly (you can rename it in Site settings).

PUT IT ONLINE — VERCEL (free)
1. Unzip this file.
2. Go to https://vercel.com/new and import the unzipped folder
   (or install Vercel CLI and run "vercel" inside the folder).

CONTACT FORM — ONE-TIME ACTIVATION
The contact form sends messages to info@Shiekhacrepair.com using FormSubmit.
The FIRST time someone submits the form, FormSubmit emails an activation
link to info@Shiekhacrepair.com — open that email and click "Activate".
After that, every form message arrives in the inbox directly.

WHATSAPP BUTTON
All WhatsApp buttons open a chat with +92 303 6253428.

EDIT TEXT / PHOTOS
- Text: edit the .html files in any text editor.
- Photos: replace files in assets/img/ (keep the same file names).
- Logo: assets/logo/ (logo-header.png, logo-footer.png).
- Phone/email/address appear in several files — use Find & Replace.

PHOTOS
- The photos in assets/img/ are free-stock photos (Pexels license, free to use),
  including AC technician photos by Jose Andres Pacheco Cortes.
- Replace them with your own workshop photos anytime (keep the same file names).

SAMPLE NUMBERS & REVIEWS
The stats (500+ clients etc.) and customer reviews are placeholders.
Replace them with your real numbers and real reviews before going live.

WORDPRESS VERSION
If you want this same design inside WordPress (editable in WP admin),
use the separate file: shiekh-ac-repair-theme.zip
(Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload Theme). See its README.txt.

SEO — ALREADY BUILT IN
- Every page has its own title tag, meta description and Open Graph tags,
  written around what people actually search: "AC repair Faisalabad",
  "AC installation Faisalabad", "AC gas refilling", "AC service".
- Home page includes LocalBusiness (HVAC) structured data with your phone,
  address, areas served and hours — this helps Google show your business
  for local searches.
- FAQs page includes FAQ structured data (can appear as rich results).
- sitemap.xml and robots.txt are included. After deploying:
  1. In sitemap.xml and robots.txt, replace https://shiekhacrepair.com
     with your real domain (if different).
  2. Go to Google Search Console (search.google.com/search-console),
     add your site, and submit the sitemap URL.
  3. The content is written in a natural, human voice with keywords used
     normally in headings and text — this is what Google rewards. Do not
     stuff extra keywords into the pages; that is what gets penalized.
